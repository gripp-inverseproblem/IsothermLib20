# Isotherm++ 2.0 documentation identity

The circular scientific symbol with three adsorption curves is definitive.
The `iL` monogram is obsolete. Both marks in `_static` have transparent
backgrounds. The sidebar white logo must sit directly on the indigo sidebar
without a white or indigo backing rectangle. The light-background symbol
must show a **complete, uncropped outer circumference**, without the extra
small circle in its background.

Official colours: indigo `#27316A`, turquoise `#087F82`, coral `#DF735F`
and lilac `#E6EAF5`. Use rounded cards, circular section markers, Australian
English, clear mathematical typesetting, clear vector scientific plots and good
contrast. Align section titles vertically with their numbered circles.

The approved HTML/CSS prototype is retained separately from the source
package. The current HTML and A4 PDF are generated from the same Sphinx RST
source tree, with WeasyPrint for print layout. Visual fidelity must be checked
against the separately archived approved prototype.
